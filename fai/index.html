Case study / Court power

Judges Run Amok is the application’s legal spine.

FAI says courts will define the frontier. Zoe has already worked where the court’s administrative power became part of the constitutional problem.

problem

The judicial branch controlled payment and panel status for lawyers appointed to defend indigent people in federal court.

move

Zoe challenged the structure, appealed, documented the conflict publicly, and framed defense independence as institutional design.

outcome
